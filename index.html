OmniSenMo estimates 3D human pose as sensors change and go missing, and is compared with LEO-Fuse and X-Fi. It understands actions next to HoloLLM given the same sensors, predicts motion, and is pretrained in simulation.

Summary.

OmniSenMo turns each sensor into joint tokens grounded in the human skeleton, so that a unified language model estimates, understands, and predicts human motion from any combination of RGB, depth, LiDAR, mmWave, and WiFi. The results on this page are on actions absent from the real training data.

Method.

Skeleton-grounded joint tokens

Each sensor is turned into 17 joint tokens supervised by the 3D positions and motion tokens of the joints, so that any combination of sensors can be fused joint by joint.

Multi-Granularity Observation

Estimation takes a dense window, understanding a sparse sample, and motion prediction a past window, all within one context of the language model.

Multisensory sim-to-real pretraining

The whole model is pretrained on fully paired simulated data of all five sensors before it is adapted to real data.
